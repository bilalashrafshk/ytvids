# It's Probably Nothing — Standing Assets (Public-Health Print)

*The look, locked from the Episode 01 style test (owner: "like this style more", 2026-10-02). Replaces the earlier realistic-rooms-plus-vector-Dennis look.*

**Look:** a 1950s public-health education pamphlet, screen-printed. Flat mid-century shapes, confident deep-navy ink outlines, limited-palette screen print, visible halftone dots, slightly misregistered colour layers, soft paper grain on pale sage paper. Calm, orderly and wholesome on the surface, sitting uneasily beside what is happening. Dennis, every room and every explainer plate are drawn in the same print so nothing looks pasted on.

**Palette (draft, hexes to lock):**

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | Pale sage | `#E6EDE4` |
| Ink / keyline plate | Deep navy | `#1F2A44` |
| Dennis shirt | Mustard | `#D9A21B` |
| Dennis trousers | Teal | `#1F7A8C` |
| The disease (nothing else) | Clinical red | `#C8332B` |
| Drained state | Neutral grey | `#9A9A94` |

**Semantic lock:** clinical red is the disease and only the disease. Full colour in register means well. Grey with colour layers out of register means ill.

**The two progress devices (free in post, no extra generation):**
1. **Colour plates drop away.** As Dennis gets sicker, the printed colour layers fade out one at a time until only the navy line plate remains. The healthy road stays at full colour.
2. **Misregistration as a progress bar.** The colour layers slide further out of alignment with the ink line as the illness advances. A healthy Dennis is perfectly in register.

**Standing prompt clause (every still and AI video):**
> *1950s public-health education pamphlet illustration, flat mid-century modern shapes, confident deep-navy ink outlines, limited-palette screen print, visible halftone dot texture, slightly misregistered colour layers, soft paper grain on a pale sage-green paper base. Palette only: pale sage paper, deep navy, mustard yellow, teal, neutral grey, and one small clinical-red accent used only for the disease. Calm, wholesome, orderly tone that sits uneasily next to what is happening.*

**Standing negative prompt:** *photorealism, 3D render, glossy CGI, anime, modern flat corporate vector, white empty background, cream paper, stick figures, neon colours, heavy gradients, clutter, misspelled words, extra fingers, distorted faces, a different-looking Dennis.* There is deliberately no ban on hard imagery: the channel can show what the disease does.

**Modes (per beat):**
- **Story:** a mid-century room (kitchen, clinic, cabin, waiting room), Dennis in the print style.
- **Explainer plate:** pale sage ground, one to three objects, textbook-plate layout, stage tags ("Week 2"). Clinical red only on the virus.
- **Split:** two roads in one frame, left drained and out of register, right full colour and in register.

**Dennis Fine:** early forties, heavyset and soft (broad shoulders, round belly, full cheeks, a light double chin, side-parted brown hair, thick expressive brows, kind eyes), so the colour drain and the weight of illness read on a body with room to lose. Master portrait generated first (front, three-quarter, side, four expressions); every later Dennis uses it as the reference. Prompts: `videos/its-probably-nothing/01-rabies/08_STYLE_TEST_PROMPTS.md` (still 1).

**Distinct from the other channels (isolation check):** no shared accent hex, no borrowed characters or identity terms, no host figure. Palette and paper differ on purpose.
