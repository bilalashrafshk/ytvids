The clearest experiment comes from a 2015 study by Chirag Mittal and Vladus Griskevicius at the University of Minnesota, published in the Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. They took adults from stable childhoods and adults from unpredictable childhoods and tested two mental abilities.

Inhibition, which is the ability to hold still and block out distraction, and shifting, which is the ability to drop one task and jump cleanly to another.
