## Mandatory Voice Direction Rules & Pre-Flight Checks
1. **Two-Part Control Instruction Protocol**: Every chunk prepends the locked Persona Anchor (`"Sharp, fast-paced scenario guide, engaging, articulate, and vivid."`) and modulates only the dynamic delivery register via `"Speaks with..."`.
2. **Audio Identity Stability**: The Persona Anchor guarantees text embedding consistency across independent stateless inference calls, preventing clone degradation.
3. **Phonetic Decimal Normalization**: Zero raw numeric decimals exist in target text. Numbers like `10 point 6 million`, `24 point 5 billion dollars`, `0 point 19 standard deviations` are pre-normalized to avoid neural models reading periods as "dot".
4. **No Foley or Code Tags**: All inline stage directions (`[WATERMARK]`, `[DATA]`, `[MAP]`, `[REMOTION]`) have been stripped from the narration stream.
5. **Restraint Over Rate**: Delivery shifts are incremental from day to day to preserve continuous acoustic realism.
