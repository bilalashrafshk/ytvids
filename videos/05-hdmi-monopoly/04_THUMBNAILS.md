# Phase 04: Thumbnails — Episode 05

> Built only from the **locked title** ("Who Gets Paid Every Time You Plug In a TV?"), the thesis and the premise. Never from the script's scenes. All four are static illustrations, 3840×2160, in the channel's clean flat-vector, soft cel-shaded system. No real person appears. The HDMI logo is not used: the plug's shape carries the subject, so there is nothing for the image model to distort.
>
> **Palette (Style Bible §3):** light, neutral, greige-and-white backgrounds; one deliberate accent. Here the accent is **gold**, the coin, used once as the focal point and never with green as a co-dominant colour.
>
> **Export:** keep the 3840×2160 source, export 1280×720 for upload, and read it at 168px wide before finalising. Filenames: `000_thumbnail_A.png` to `000_thumbnail_D.png`.
>
> **Text note:** the title already asks "who". So the on-image text never repeats it: it adds a number, or it adds nothing. If "¢" renders badly, use "4 CENTS".

## Option E — "The Toll" (layout L1, annotated hero; added with the new reference-based template)

**Idea:** a giant flat HDMI plug plugged into the back of a TV, with three hand-drawn labels and arrows pointing at it, and the small TV builder standing beside it looking up with a worried frown.

**Why it gets the click:** the TV is the anchor (everyone owns one); the wrong thing is the price tag on a plug ("4¢ A TV") plus the label "YOU NEVER SEE IT". The worried builder makes the viewer ask who is being charged.

**Checks (`bible/03`):**
- **Anchor / wrong thing:** anchor = the back of a TV with a cable in it; wrong thing = it carries a price. The plug alone is a borderline anchor, so the TV is drawn in the frame.
- **Legibility:** headline "THE HDMI TOLL?!", three words, longest word 6 characters, heavy condensed caps; read it at 168 px before finalising.
- **Callouts:** three, all plain English. "4¢ A TV" rests on the fee tiers the audit marks "as widely reported" (02_AUDIT item 1), so keep that wording in the description.
- **Subject-bridge:** the headline plus the plug and TV name the subject without a data badge.
- **Closest benchmark:** The Business Vault, "How Five-Star Hotels Make Money" (43.1x, 578,605 views); Tony Talks Costco (16,567 views) is supporting evidence only and Mr Finance is unvalidated.
- **Palette:** warm greige ground, slate-grey TV, one gold accent (the plug contacts), one deep-amber underline. Follows the episode's Theme Lock, not red.
- **Not a copy:** leans on Tony Talks / Business Vault layout conventions only. Three differences: the hero is a plug on a TV, not a storefront or building; the accent is gold on greige, not red on blue grid; the character is our own small builder, not a blocky mascot.
