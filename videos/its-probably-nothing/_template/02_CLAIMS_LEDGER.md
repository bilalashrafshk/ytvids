# Claims Ledger

Every medical claim, number, timeline and mechanism in the script has a row. Checked by `python3 gate_claims.py <script> 02_CLAIMS_LEDGER.md --metadata 13_FINAL_METADATA.md`.
Tier: **A** guideline/systematic review/major health body | **B** peer-reviewed human study | **C** preclinical or preliminary (script must hedge) | **N** invented story detail (no source; never a medical claim).
Source must include a URL, DOI or PMID (except tier N).
