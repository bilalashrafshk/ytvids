# Phase 04: Thumbnail Packaging & Concepts
## Episode 03: Social Media Blackout for 30 Days

> **Instructions**: Standalone packaging deliverable. Generate 4K thumbnail concepts mapping across the proven Outlier Archetypes from the Master Channel Bible. Options A–D are the original 4; **Option E was added after editorial rejection of A–D** and built to lead directly from the locked master title.
>
> **LATEST ENGINE GATE AUDIT:**
> 1. **Standardized Filename Convention:** `000_thumbnail_A.png`, `000_thumbnail_B.png`, `000_thumbnail_C.png`, `000_thumbnail_D.png`.
> 2. **Instant-Recognition Object Rule:** Every concept names its hero object, passes the 1-second legibility test, and enforces the **Subject-Bridge Check** (connecting the digital social platform directly to the physical trade consequence without inferential distance).
> 3. **The Anchor Rule (mandatory):** Every concept needs one nameable-by-anyone anchor (a phone, a house, a brand, a dollar figure) plus one "wrong thing." Industry-specific objects — a demurrage ticket, a shipping container, an ad-auction gavel, a gate terminal — may only ever be the "wrong thing," never the anchor itself.
> 4. **Benchmark Cross-Check:** Every concept names its specific proof-point benchmark from `references/COMPETITOR_METADATA_AND_THUMBNAIL_AUDIT.md`, cited with its verified view count.
> 5. **The Legibility Rule (replaces the 1–4 word cap):** Tested via 168px cap-height / arm's-length readability, not word count. No single word exceeds 11 characters. One line, or two with size hierarchy — never three. Zero words is valid.
> 6. **Callout Density (replaces the flat 2–4 badge cap):** Set by archetype — isometric/infographic needs 8–15 callouts, character/object-staging needs 0–2, split-comparison needs 0. Every callout must be priceable by someone with zero industry knowledge.
> 7. **Universal Quality Clause:** Verbatim `"follow best industry-standard guidelines and quality and visualisations"` included in every prompt.
>
> **Logo/Brand Rule Correction:** Options A, B, and C previously used a generic, unbranded "dead social app icon." Per the Master Channel Bible's Logo/Brand Rule (Section 8): "when a real, named company or platform is the episode's actual subject... its real logo/wordmark may appear in the thumbnail as a literal, undistorted graphic element... withholding it in favor of an abstract metaphor is a legibility failure, not a safety measure." Meta/Facebook is the episode's real named subject (`02_SCRIPT.md:40,160,271`), so those three now show the real Facebook (and, on Option A, Instagram) logo, literal and undistorted, grayed to monochrome to signal "offline." **Option D is the exception**: its "Sign In With Google" text was originally correct — grounded in a distinct, specifically-sourced script beat about the port gate's own Google-based SSO (`02_SCRIPT.md:243,71`; `01_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md:67,115`), separate from the Facebook/Instagram consumer feeds. A prior pass incorrectly swapped it to "Facebook," conflating the two real mechanisms; that has been reverted.
