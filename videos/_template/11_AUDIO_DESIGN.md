# Phase 11: Batch 4 — Audio Score & Sound Design

> **Instructions**: Standalone audio production deliverable. Dedicated score bed prompts (Suno/Udio), tactile foley trigger specifications, and dynamic NLE volume ducking envelopes.
>
> **MANDATORY RULES:**
> 1. **Cohesive Score Bed:** 1 master cohesive musical theme per episode (or calibrated act movements), strictly instrumental with NO vocals/choir.
> 2. **Dynamic Ducking Architecture:** Base BGM ducked to `-32dB to -35dB` during narration; cognitive ducking to `-50dB to -60dB` during dense math/charts; pause swells of `+8dB` during narration pauses $> 1.2\text{s}$; instant silence cuts on shock reveals.
> 3. **Tactile Foley:** Concrete paper slides, sub-bass 40Hz drops, and mechanical clicks aligned to visual transitions.
