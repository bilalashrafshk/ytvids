# Phase 04: Thumbnail Packaging & Concepts

> **Instructions**: Standalone packaging deliverable. Generate 3 to 6 distinct 4K thumbnail concepts (Options A through F) across the 7 thumbnail styles (T1–T7) defined in `bible/03-thumbnail-style.md`. Apply the Anchor Rule, Legibility Rule, Callout Density, Instant-Recognition Object Rule and Benchmark Cross-Check from that file.
>
> **MANDATORY RULES:**
> 1. **Legibility Rule (replaces the old 1–4 word cap):** headline must read at 168px wide — one line (or two with clear size hierarchy), no word over 11 characters, weight 700+, high contrast. Zero words is allowed when the image carries the idea.
> 2. **Callout Density by style:** isometric/infographic (T7) may carry many; character or object staging usually one big number or none; split comparison usually none. Every callout priceable with zero industry knowledge (`$65 A YEAR`, not `DEMURRAGE: $275/DAY`).
> 3. **Prompt Quality Clause:** Every prompt MUST include verbatim: `"follow best industry-standard guidelines and quality and visualisations"`.
> 4. **Text Specification:** Every prompt MUST declare: `ON-IMAGE TEXT: Direct in-generation text: "[exact text]"`.
